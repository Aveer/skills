# Domain overlays

Load only the relevant domain guidance.

## Manga / anime / visual novels

Always consider:

- best medium for this user, not merely the canonical/original medium;
- adaptation completeness and divergence;
- audiovisual strengths;
- reading/layout friction;
- route/internal-monologue dependence for VNs;
- current regional availability.

Possible paths include:

- anime only;
- anime then manga continuation;
- manga from the beginning;
- VN as the definitive version;
- sample one medium first, then switch.

Do not assume reading volume itself is negative unless the user profile says so.

## Films / TV

Consider:

- central narrative engine vs episodic/vibe-first structure;
- ending/payoff quality;
- atmosphere and visual identity;
- season-to-season degradation;
- mystery-box stalling;
- runtime.

A film can succeed on atmosphere or one strong idea more easily than a long
series, so apply long-form penalties carefully.

## Games

Do not reduce game taste to genre labels.

Potentially stronger predictors include:

- level/spatial/world design;
- systemic freedom/reactivity;
- atmosphere and inhabitation;
- exploration/discovery;
- mechanical mastery/flow;
- player agency;
- world interconnectivity;
- tactical depth;
- simulation/economy depth;
- environmental storytelling;
- narrative reactivity/payoff;
- friction/jank tolerance;
- generic/checklist penalties.

Also evaluate **state recommendation**:

- play now;
- wait for patches/DLC/definitive edition;
- choose another platform;
- use a particular edition/version.

A user's tolerance for technical roughness and desire for an optimal first
playthrough should come from the profile, not from this generic skill.
